Admissions

Pathways into the five-year B.Eng and postgraduate Computer Engineering programmes at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria.

Undergraduate

B.Eng Computer Engineering (5 years)

Admission into the undergraduate programme follows Ahmadu Bello University and Faculty of Engineering regulations. Candidates typically present UTME / Direct Entry credentials acceptable to the University, with subject combinations suited to Engineering, and must meet departmental screening and cut-off requirements published for each session.

The programme runs for five academic years under the semester system, incorporating industrial exposure through schemes such as SIWES as prescribed by the Faculty.

ABU Admissions / Student Portal Programme overview

Postgraduate

PGD, M.Sc & Ph.D routes

Postgraduate admissions are processed through the School of Postgraduate Studies. Applicants should consult current ABU postgraduate advertisements for entry requirements, fees, and deadlines.

PGD

Postgraduate Diploma

Suitable for candidates seeking a structured bridge or professional development pathway into Computer Engineering at postgraduate level.

M.Sc

Master of Science

Typically requires a good honours degree in Computer Engineering or a closely related field from a recognised university, plus successful completion of coursework and a supervised thesis.

Ph.D

Doctor of Philosophy

For candidates with appropriate master’s-level preparation ready to undertake original research under departmental supervision.

PG Portal / ABU website

Specialization note

Within the B.Eng programme, students choose specialization modules at 400 Level and 500 Level from:

  • AI Engineering
  • Control Engineering
  • Networking Engineering

Earlier levels follow the common Computer Engineering curriculum before specialization.

How to apply (overview)

  1. Monitor official ABU undergraduate or postgraduate admission announcements for the relevant session.
  2. Complete the University application via the Student Portal or Postgraduate Portal as applicable (abu.edu.ng).
  3. Meet Faculty/Departmental screening and documentation requirements.
  4. For programme-specific questions, contact the Department at comengg@abu.edu.ng.
