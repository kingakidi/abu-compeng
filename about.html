About the Department

Computer Engineering at ABU

From a shared engineering heritage to an autonomous department educating the next generation of computer engineers.

History

A lineage of engineering excellence

Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria at its creation in 1962 inherited the Engineering programme from the Nigeria College of Arts, Science, and Technology, Zaria. Degrees in Electrical, Mechanical and Civil Engineering were awarded under the auspices of the University of London from 1962 to 1965. The Faculty of Engineering and its founding departments became fully autonomous in 1965.

For many years the Department of Electrical Engineering offered a three-year Bachelor of Engineering degree. With SWEP and SIWES, programmes moved to four years by the 1981/82 session. By 1991/92 the University adopted the semester system, and Faculty degree programmes became five-year B.Eng programmes.

During the 2010/11 session, after a Resource Assessment exercise, the National Universities Commission approved renaming Electrical Engineering to Electrical and Computer Engineering, and subsequently approved splitting into Electrical Engineering; Communications Engineering (now Electronics and Telecommunications); and Computer Engineering. These departments were given full autonomy in November 2017.

The Computer Engineering programme is based on the NUC Benchmark Minimum Academic Standards (BMAS), international best practices, and the competencies required to compete in a global ICT, AI, networking, and intelligent-systems ecosystem.

Mission

Train high-level manpower for effective service delivery across computer engineering domains — including intelligent systems, networked infrastructures, and digital technologies — for sustainable national development.

Vision

Produce high-quality computer engineers who design, manage, and advance computing and cyber-physical systems in harmony with society and the environment, competing favourably with leading Computer Engineering programmes worldwide.

Philosophy

“The first duty of every University is the search for and the spread of knowledge and the establishment of truths … But it must also serve the needs of the nation.” — Sir Ahmadu Bello, first ABU convocation, 1963

ABU’s philosophy rests on imparting knowledge to men and women of all races without distinction on grounds of race, religious or political beliefs. The Department likewise imparts Computer Engineering knowledge and skills at all levels — without such distinctions — to serve Nigeria and humanity.

Programme Educational Objectives

PEO1 – PEO5

What graduates are expected to achieve in the years following completion of the programme.

  • PEO1 — Professional Competence and Problem Solving Apply computer engineering principles, analytical skills, and modern tools to real-world problems in industry, government, and society.
  • PEO2 — Lifelong Learning and Professional Development Pursue lifelong learning, postgraduate study, research, and emerging innovations in computer engineering.
  • PEO3 — Innovation and Sustainable Technology Development Design sustainable, research-driven solutions in embedded systems, AI, robotics, cybersecurity, and intelligent computing.
  • PEO4 — Ethics, Leadership and Communication Demonstrate ethical responsibility, leadership, teamwork, and clear communication in multidisciplinary settings.
  • PEO5 — Entrepreneurship and Societal Impact Contribute to national and global technological development through entrepreneurship and locally relevant applications.

Programme Outcomes

PO1 – PO12 (condensed)

Graduate attributes spanning engineering knowledge through lifelong learning.

  • PO1 Engineering Knowledge Apply mathematics, science, and engineering fundamentals to complex problems.
  • PO2 Problem Analysis Identify, formulate, and analyze complex engineering problems.
  • PO3 Design / Development of Solutions Design systems and processes with societal and environmental care.
  • PO4 Investigation Conduct research-based investigations and draw valid conclusions.
  • PO5 Modern Tool Usage Select and apply modern engineering and ICT tools appropriately.
  • PO6 The Engineer and Society Reason about societal, health, safety, legal, and cultural issues.
  • PO7 Environment and Sustainability Understand impacts and the need for sustainable development.
  • PO8 Ethics Commit to professional ethics and COREN codes of conduct.
  • PO9 Individual and Team Work Work effectively alone and in diverse teams.
  • PO10 Communication Communicate clearly with engineers and the wider public.
  • PO11 Project Management Apply engineering and management principles to projects.
  • PO12 Lifelong Learning Engage in independent learning amid technological change.
