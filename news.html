Women Leading Change: Spotlight on Departmental Leadership

The Department of Computer Engineering recognises leadership that advances inclusion in engineering education. Under Head of Department Dr. Engr. Mrs. Basira Yahaya, the Department continues to support women-in-STEM conversations across the Faculty of Engineering and to model professional excellence for undergraduate and postgraduate cohorts.

This spotlight affirms the Department’s commitment to equitable participation in teaching, research, and professional formation.

1 November 2025 Anniversary

Department Marks Autonomy Anniversary

Computer Engineering reflects on full departmental autonomy granted in November 2017. Since then, the Department has strengthened teaching, research, and industry-aligned specializations in AI Engineering, Control Engineering, and Networking Engineering.

The anniversary is a reminder of the Department’s roots in ABU’s engineering heritage and its ongoing mission to prepare graduates for a global computing ecosystem.
